# Superstore Sales Analysis

I used Python and pandas to clean and explore a retail sales dataset of 51,290 orders from 2011 to 2015, and tested five questions about sales and profit.

## What I did
I removed columns I didn't need, fixed the date formats, and made charts for each question. The full work is in the notebook.

## What I found
- Technology had the best profit margin (about 14%), just ahead of Office Supplies.
- Central was the top region for sales. East was near the bottom, so my guess that East would lead was wrong.
- Sales are highest in November and December.
- Weekdays make much more profit than weekends.
- Tables lose money overall.
